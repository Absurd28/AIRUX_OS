AIROX Connect

Use AIRA, the Task Manager, files and your screen from your Android phone or tablet.

Allow phone connections

Phones on this network can connect after you pair them.

Pair a phone

Open AIROX Connect on the phone, tap Pair with a PC and scan the code. You confirm here.

Paired phones and tablets

None yet.

Get the app

Install AIROX Connect on Android straight from this PC (for 10 minutes, on this network only).

Options

AIRA answers spoken questions from the phone on the phone only (not out loud on the PC)
Show all PC notifications on the phone (otherwise only AIRA, Phagos, Drivers and Task Manager)
Phones connect only from this local network, over TLS 1.3 to this PC's own certificate. Every action from a phone passes AIRA's permission broker: deleting, ending programs, sending files off the PC and power actions are confirmed on the phone, and everything is written to AIRA's action log. The second screen and touchpad need your one-time OK in KDE's "Screen Sharing" / "Remote control" window.
